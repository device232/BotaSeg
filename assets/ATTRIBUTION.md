# Figure provenance

The PNG files in this directory are paper figures supplied by the project
authors and copied from the manuscript working materials for this code
release. They are included to document the method and reported results:

- `phenotyping-workflow.png`: organ-instance to phenotype-fingerprint workflow.
- `qualitative-comparison.png`: qualitative semantic and instance comparison.
- `trait-agreement.png`: out-of-fold prediction-to-annotation trait agreement.

Do not reuse these figures independently of the accompanying paper without
the authors' permission.
